description: What counts as evidence of a durable competitive advantage
---
A moat needs evidence, not adjectives:
- Strong: stable or rising gross margins over 5 years with low variance,
  documented switching costs or network effects, patents or licences that
  block competitors.
- Weak: marketing language in the 10-K ("leading", "innovative"), a single
  good year, or high margins in a cyclical upswing.
Name the specific moat type and the number that supports it. Without one, stay neutral.
