description: How to weigh the DCF scenarios, owner earnings and the EV/EBITDA method
---
Weigh the valuation methods before deciding:
- Anchor on the base-case DCF; treat bear and bull as the range, not as targets.
- When the methods disagree by more than 2x, trust the cash-flow based ones
  (DCF, owner earnings) over the EV/EBITDA multiple and lower your confidence.
- A margin of safety below 20% is not a buy signal, however strong the growth story.
- If free cash flow was replaced by a fallback figure, say so and cap confidence at 60.
