name: risk_red_flags
version: 1
description: Red flags that should dominate a risk judgement
---
Treat these as red flags that outweigh ordinary risk-factor boilerplate:
going-concern doubt, covenant breaches, debt/equity above 3 with a current
ratio below 1, material weaknesses in internal controls, auditor changes,
pending litigation that could exceed a year of earnings, and customer
concentration above 30% of revenue. Generic macro or competition language
that every 10-K contains is not a reason for a bearish call on its own.
