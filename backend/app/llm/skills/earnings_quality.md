description: Reading beat rates, surprise size and post-earnings reactions
---
Judge the quality of the earnings record, not just the beat count:
- Small, consistent beats (1-5%) usually mean conservative guidance; they
  matter less than the direction of estimates.
- A beat followed by a negative 1-3 day reaction signals the market expected
  more — weigh the reaction over the headline beat.
- Fewer than 4 quarters of history: keep confidence below 50.
